# Privacy Policy for RAF - Restock at Fern's

**Last updated: 10 October 2026**

---

RAF - Restock at Fern's ("RAF", "the game", "we", "us") is a mobile game published by an individual developer on Google Play.
This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The game is free to play. It shows advertising, and it offers two optional in-app purchases.

## The short version

- **There are no accounts.** You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or
  any other detail that identifies you personally.
- **Your progress stays on your phone.** Levels completed, coins and the shop fit-outs you have bought
  are stored in the game's own storage on the device. Deleting the game deletes
  them. There is no cloud save.
- **We do not sell your data**, and we do not build a profile of you ourselves.
- Advertising and analytics are handled by **Unity**, and they do collect information. That is what
  the rest of this policy is about.

## What is collected, and by whom

### Advertising — Unity Ads / LevelPlay

Advertising in the game is served through **Unity LevelPlay**, Unity's ad mediation service, which
may in turn request ads from other advertising networks it works with.

To show ads, and to measure whether they worked, these services may collect:

- your device's **advertising identifier** (on Android, the Google Advertising ID) and other device
  identifiers;
- **device and network information** — model, operating system version, language, network type, and
  your **IP address**, from which an approximate (city- or country-level) location can be derived;
- **information about the ads themselves** — which ads were shown, whether you watched or skipped
  one, and whether you tapped it.

Unity acts as an independent controller of this information. What it does with it is described in
Unity's own policy: <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings
(**Settings → Google → Ads** on most Android phones), which breaks the link between you and any
advertising profile built from it.

### Analytics — Unity Analytics

The game sends **Unity Analytics** a small set of events about how the game itself is played, so we
can tell which levels are too hard and whether the game is worth continuing to make. These are
events such as: a level was started, won or lost and which number it was; a booster was used and how it was paid
for; an ad was offered and how it ended; an interstitial ad was shown; a shop fit-out was bought; a
purchase completed. They are accompanied by standard technical
information — device model, operating system, and an identifier for the installation.

**None of these events contains anything you have typed or anything that identifies you as a person**,
because the game never asks you for anything.

### Purchases

If you buy "Remove ads" or a coin pack, the payment is handled entirely by **Google Play**. We never
see or receive your card details, your address or your name. We receive only confirmation from Google
that a purchase for this app completed, so the game can give you what you bought.

## Your choices

### Consent, where the law requires it

If you are in a region whose law requires it — the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and
certain other jurisdictions — the game will show you a consent form the first time you open it,
before any advertising or analytics data is collected. You can consent, refuse with **Do not
consent**, or choose **Manage options** to decide purpose by purpose, and **refusing does not restrict
the game in any way**: you will still see ads, they will simply not be personalised.

The form is provided by **Google's User Messaging Platform**, and your choice is passed on to Unity
and to the advertising networks in the mediation. **You can change your answer at any time from the
game itself**: open the shop beside your coins in the Market and choose **Privacy settings**. That button appears wherever the consent form does.

### Opting out of personalised ads

Independently of the above, you can turn off ad personalisation for every app on your device through
your device settings, and you can reset your advertising identifier there at any time.

### Removing ads

Buying "Remove ads" stops all advertising in the game. Note that this stops the *ads*; it does not by
itself stop the analytics described above.

## Children

**This game is not directed at children.** It is not designed for, marketed to, or intended for
children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through this game, please
contact us at the address below and we will delete what we can.

## How long information is kept

We do not operate servers of our own and we do not retain a copy of anything described here. The
retention periods that apply are those of the services doing the collecting — Unity and Google — and
are set out in their own policies, linked above.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to
have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. Because the game holds nothing about you
itself, most such requests are best directed to Unity or Google, whose policies are linked above.

If you would like help with that, or you want to raise anything else about this policy, write to us
and we will do what we can.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.
Material changes will also be noted in the game's Google Play listing.
